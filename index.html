How to play

You get one second of a Laufey song. Guess it, or skip — either way the clip grows.

  1. Clip lengths: 1s → 2s → 4s → 7s → 11s → 16s
  2. Six tries. A wrong guess or a skip unlocks the next length.
  3. Daily gives everyone the same song, changing at midnight. Endless is random, forever.
  4. Stuck? Reveal first word shows the first word of the title. It costs 25 points and tags your win with &*.
  5. Solving faster scores more: 100 / 80 / 60 / 45 / 30 / 20 points by clip length. Runs are ranked on the leaderboard (♔).

Clips come from Apple's previews, so they usually start at the hook rather than the first note. Consider it a feature.
