How to play

You get one second of a Laufey song. Guess it, or skip — either way the clip grows.

  1. Clip lengths: 1s → 2s → 4s → 7s → 11s → 16s
  2. Six tries. A wrong guess or a skip unlocks the next length.
  3. Daily gives everyone the same song, changing at midnight. Endless is random, forever.
  4. Insane ^ shrinks the ladder to 0.1 → 0.5 → 0.6 → 1 → 1.2 → 1.5 → 2s across seven tries, pays roughly triple, and swaps in the Lofi Versions where they exist — same answer, different arrangement. Wins there are tagged ^.
  5. Laufi ~ is lofi only, and mixes her four Lofi Versions with soundalikes from the same lane — Lauren Rose, Samara Joy, Melody Gardot and others. Half the rounds aren't Laufey at all. It pays the most and wins are tagged ~.
  6. Insane and Laufi give you no hints.
  7. Stuck? Reveal first letters shows the opening letters of the title — never a whole word, so you still have to place it. Costs 25 points and tags your win with &*.
  8. Solving faster scores more: 100 / 80 / 60 / 45 / 30 / 20 points by clip length. Runs are ranked on the leaderboard (♔).

Clips come from Apple's previews, so they usually start at the hook rather than the first note. Consider it a feature.
